Statement puller

A private tool for one person: it files the owner's own emailed statements.

What it is

Statement puller is a small personal script written and used by Maulin Tolia for his own income-tax bookkeeping. Banks, a credit-card issuer and a broker email him monthly statements and contract notes as attachments. Statement puller looks in his Gmail account for emails from a short list of those senders and saves their attachments into his own tax-records folders on his own computer.

It is not a public service. It has no sign-up, no other users and no customer data. The only account it ever connects to is its owner's.

How it uses Google data

  • It asks for one Google permission: read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • It downloads only attachments from the senders on the owner's list, and only to the owner's own storage.
  • It does not share, sell or advertise with this data.

The details are in the Privacy Policy.
