Privacy Policy

Statement puller · effective 21 November 2026

Who this covers

Statement puller (home page) is a private, single-user tool operated by Maulin Tolia (the “owner”). It is used only by the owner, on the owner's own Google account and the owner's own computer. It is not offered to anyone else, and it collects nothing about any other person.

What data the app accesses

  • Gmail messages and their attachments, read-only (the gmail.readonly permission), limited in practice to emails from a short list of senders chosen by the owner, such as his banks, credit-card issuer and broker.
  • The owner's Google account email address, shown once at sign-in to confirm which mailbox was connected.

The app cannot send, delete or modify email.

How the data is used

Matching attachments (statements, contract notes and similar documents) are saved into the owner's own tax-records folders so the owner can prepare his income-tax return. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling, selling or any other purpose. Message text is not read for any purpose other than finding the sender, subject and attachments.

Where the data is stored

Saved files and the app's sign-in token stay on the owner's own computer. The token is kept in a file that only the owner's user account can read. No copy is sent to the app's author or to any third party by the Gmail connector.

Sharing and transfers

The app does not sell, rent or share Google user data. The owner's other personal tools may later open the saved files, for example to read a statement or to total transactions. If the owner chooses to use a transcription service for that, such as the Anthropic Claude API under his own account, that is the owner processing his own documents by his own choice, and it is not done by the Gmail connector.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Statement puller's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Saved files stay until the owner deletes them. The owner can stop the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the stored sign-in token from his computer.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is not used by anyone other than its owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
